Connect to Network

Connect your computer to ONE device via console, then start the run.
① Physical Setup (you do this)
  1. Rack, cable, and power on all your network devices.
  2. Connect any one device's console port to your computer's USB/Serial.
  3. Wait for the device to finish booting (typically 30–90 seconds).
② Console Port
Select the COM port or tty of your USB-serial adapter.
The platform will probe this first, then fallback candidates.
③ Options
Without this, the run is dry-run / staging only.
