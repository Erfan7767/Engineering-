# Open Items Register

The ONLY place where spec questions, deviations, and gaps open or close.
No item closes without a recorded decision (and, where applicable, an ADR
or harness evidence). Traceability between releases is maintained here.

Format: `OI-NNNN | status | opened (phase) | decision-owner | description → decision/evidence`.
Statuses: OPEN · DECIDED · CLOSED (evidence attached) · SUPERSEDED.
